7. Use of the Motor Vehicle

7.1. Commencement of driving the Motor Vehicle

Before starting a drive, a Motor Vehicle must be reserved in the RESS App. Initial reservation time is free. Upon reaching the vehicle, select the command to unlock it. The User must inspect interior and exterior condition. Any unlabelled, pre-existing damages must be notified to RESS immediately via the contact details in the app before starting the trip.

7.2. Operating Area, finishing the drive and returning the Motor Vehicle

The User can start and return the Motor Vehicle only in the Operating Area as indicated on RESS App. To terminate the Period of Use:

  • park publicly in compliance with traffic regulations;
  • turn off engine, place key in designated place, close all windows and doors;
  • clean up after yourself and ensure documents and Fuel/Charging card remain in the vehicle;
  • choose “Finish ride” on the RESS App and verify the vehicle is locked.

Parking in underground garages, barrier-secured areas, private yards, or prohibited parking zones is strictly forbidden.

7.3. Refuelling and recharging the Motor Vehicle

A Fuel Card or Charging Card is kept inside each vehicle. It may only be used for that specific Motor Vehicle. The User must never return the vehicle with an empty tank (≤ 10% fuel) or depleted battery (≤ 20%). Using the fuel card for other containers or vehicles is misappropriation and will be reported to the police.

8. Handling of Accidents, Damage, Defects and Repairs

If the vehicle breaks down, unusual noises occur, or warning signals light up, stop immediately and notify RESS by telephone. In case of accident, theft, fire, or damage, immediately contact public authorities (police, fire service) and RESS. Complete an accident declaration and preserve evidence. Never admit liability without RESS consent. RESS alone is entitled to select repair shops.
