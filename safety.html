Safety comes first.

RESS is designed to protect you before, during, and after every journey. We've built industry-leading security directly into the core of our platform.

Before You Ride

Verified drivers and thorough background checks

Every single driver on the RESS platform undergoes rigorous identity verification, vehicle inspection, and comprehensive background checks before they are ever allowed to accept a trip.

You will always see your driver's photo, name, rating, and vehicle details (including the license plate) in the app before you even get in the car.

Real-time ride tracking

Share your live trip status and exact GPS location with family or friends so they can follow your journey from start to finish.

Emergency SOS Button

If you ever feel unsafe, a dedicated in-app SOS button instantly alerts your emergency contacts and dispatches RESS Security teams to your live location.

Geofencing & Safe Zones

Set designated safe areas on your map. Get automatic push notifications if a ride deviates significantly from the expected route or leaves a safe zone.

Encrypted & Protected Data

Your personal information, payment details, and exact home address are heavily encrypted. Calls between you and the driver are anonymized to protect your phone number.
