How RESS Works

From the moment you open the app to the moment you arrive, order food, or get help, we've designed every step around your convenience and safety.

1

Create your account

Download the RESS app from the App Store or Google Play. Set up your profile with a few quick details and add your preferred payment method securely.

2

Choose your service

Whether you need a quick ride, food delivery, a trusted artisan, or emergency security, tap the service you need directly from the home screen.

3

Track in real-time

See exactly where your driver, food, or service provider is on the map. We provide full details including their name, photo, and vehicle information so you know exactly who to expect.

4

Pay safely and rate

Payment is processed seamlessly through the app. Leave a rating and feedback to help us maintain the highest quality standards across the entire RESS ecosystem.
