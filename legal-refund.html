Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: 8th October, 2026 • Last Updated: 8th October, 2026

Welcome to RESS (“RESS”, “we”, “our”, or “us”). This Refund and Cancellation Policy explains the conditions under which users may cancel services and request refunds when using the RESS platform, including:

  • R-Ride (Ride-Hailing Services)
  • E-Eat (Food Delivery Services)
  • S-Security (Emergency and Security Services)
  • S-Services (On-Demand Professional and Personal Services)

By accessing or using the RESS platform, website, or mobile applications, you agree to this Refund and Cancellation Policy.

1. General Principles

  • RESS acts as a technology platform connecting users with independent drivers, merchants, vendors, security providers, and service professionals.
  • Refund eligibility depends on the type of service requested, the stage of service completion, and the circumstances surrounding the transaction.
  • All refund requests are reviewed individually and may require supporting information before approval.
  • RESS reserves the right to approve, deny, or partially approve refunds where appropriate.
  • Refunds are not intended to compensate users for circumstances beyond the reasonable control of RESS, including but not limited to traffic delays, weather conditions, network outages, force majeure events, or actions of independent service providers.

2. Payment Methods

Payments made through RESS may include:

  • Debit Cards
  • Credit Cards
  • Digital Wallets
  • Mobile Money Services
  • Bank Transfers
  • RESS Credits, Coupons, or Promotional Balances

Approved refunds will generally be returned through the original payment method whenever possible. Where this is not possible, refunds may be issued as RESS Wallet Credits, Promotional Credits, or alternative approved payment channels.

Processing times may vary depending on the financial institution involved.

3. R-Ride (Ride-Hailing Services)

3.1 Rider Cancellation Before Driver Acceptance

If a rider cancels a ride request before a driver accepts the trip, no cancellation fee will normally apply. Any payment authorization or temporary hold may be automatically released by the payment provider.

3.2 Rider Cancellation After Driver Acceptance

If a rider cancels after a driver has accepted the trip, a cancellation fee may apply. Factors considered include time elapsed since driver acceptance, driver travel distance toward pickup, waiting time, and repeated cancellations. The fee will be displayed where possible before confirmation.

3.3 Driver Cancellation

If a driver cancels the trip before pickup, the rider will not be charged. Any authorized hold will be released, and the rider may be matched with another available driver.

3.4 No-Show Situations

A rider may be charged a cancellation or waiting fee if the driver arrives at the pickup location, a reasonable waiting time has elapsed, and the rider cannot be contacted or fails to appear.

3.5 Ride Service Issues

Users may request a review where incorrect fares were charged, duplicate payments occurred, the trip was incomplete, an unreasonable route was taken, or technical errors occurred. Following investigation, RESS may provide a full or partial refund, account credit, or fare adjustment.

4. E-Eat (Food Delivery Services)

4.1 Order Cancellation Before Merchant Acceptance

Users may cancel food orders before the merchant accepts the order. Where payment has already been processed, a refund will be issued.

4.2 Order Cancellation After Merchant Acceptance

Once preparation has begun, cancellation may not be possible because food products are prepared immediately after acceptance.

4.3 Cancellation During Delivery

Orders already in transit are not eligible for cancellation, except under exceptional circumstances where partial refunds may be considered.

4.4 Missing, Incorrect, or Damaged Orders

Users should report issues promptly. RESS may request photos, order details, and evidence. Following review, RESS may issue a full or partial refund, store credit, or a replacement order where available.

4.5 Food Quality Concerns

Quality concerns should be reported promptly and will be assessed individually based on merchant verification, conditions, and evidence.

5. S-Security (Emergency and Security Services)

5.1 Emergency Response Requests

Due to the critical nature of emergency response, cancellation is not possible once emergency personnel or security response teams have already been dispatched, and charges may still apply.

5.2 False or Misuse Requests

Users are not entitled to refunds where emergency requests were submitted falsely or misused. RESS reserves the right to suspend or terminate accounts involved in misuse.

5.3 Service Availability Issues

If emergency services could not be provided due to system or provider failures, RESS will review the transaction and issue an appropriate refund or credit.

6. S-Services (On-Demand Services)

6.1 Cancellation Before Acceptance: Users may cancel without penalty before a provider accepts the booking.

6.2 Cancellation After Acceptance: Cancellation fees may apply taking into account provider travel time, materials, and reserved time.

6.3 After Commencement: Once service has begun, full refunds are generally unavailable. Partial refunds may be considered for significant deficiencies.

6.4 Provider No-Show: If a provider fails to arrive, users will receive a refund, service credits, or a replacement provider.

7. Promotions, Discounts, and Credits

Promotions, coupons, and referral credits have no cash value, are non-transferable, and may expire according to promotion terms. When a transaction is refunded, promotional credits may be restored rather than refunded as cash.

8. Duplicate or Unauthorized Charges

Users should notify RESS immediately if they suspect duplicate or unauthorized transactions. RESS will investigate and request supporting information before processing adjustments.

9. Refund Request Process

To request a refund, please contact RESS Support with your full name, registered phone number, registered email, trip or order reference number, transaction date/time, and description of the issue with supporting evidence.

10. Refund Processing Times

Approved refunds are processed promptly. Actual receipt of funds depends on your bank, card issuer, or mobile money provider and may take several business days.

11. Fraud Prevention

RESS reserves the right to deny refunds where there is evidence of fraud, policy abuse, or bad faith disputes. Accounts involved in fraud may be permanently terminated.

12. Chargebacks

Users are encouraged to contact RESS Support before initiating a chargeback. RESS reserves the right to suspend accounts under dispute and provide documentation to financial institutions.

13. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, RESS shall not be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from cancellations or payment delays. Nothing in this policy limits statutory consumer protection rights.

14. Policy Changes

RESS reserves the right to modify this policy at any time. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of updated policies.
