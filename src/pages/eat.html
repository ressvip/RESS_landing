Frequently asked questions

Clear answers regarding merchant onboarding, commissions, payouts, and delivery.

How fast can my business start selling on RESS Eat? +
Most merchants go live within 5 to 7 business days. Once you register your business and submit your catalog, our team formats your listing and activates your store profile.
What fees or commissions does RESS charge merchant partners? +
There are zero listing or registration fees to join RESS Eat. We agree on a transparent commission per fulfilled order, allowing you to grow your business without fixed operational overhead.
How do restaurant and grocery partners receive their earnings? +
All order earnings are calculated automatically and disbursed on a weekly schedule directly into your registered Nigerian corporate bank account, complete with itemized transaction reports.
Can non-restaurant businesses like supermarkets or bakeries join? +
Yes. RESS Eat supports supermarkets, bakeries, florists, pet shops, and specialty food retailers. Our platform is built for any merchant providing consumable or urgent goods to local customers.
How does RESS Eat keep food hot and secure during transit? +
Every delivery courier is equipped with insulated, weather-sealed carrier bags. Merchants seal order packaging before dispatch, ensuring your meals arrive at the right temperature and untouched.
