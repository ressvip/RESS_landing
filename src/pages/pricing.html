Our pricing commitments to Nigerian riders and households

How RESS approaches cost, commission, and consumer protection differently.

Upfront fare guarantee

The price displayed on your screen before booking is your final charge. We do not retroactively alter your fare because of standard Lagos or Abuja traffic conditions.

Surge capping during severe weather

When heavy downpours strike, foreign platforms often double or triple fares. RESS caps dynamic adjustments strictly to protect commuters while keeping drivers fairly compensated.

Escrow workmanship protection

Fees for electricians, plumbers, and home artisans remain locked safely in escrow until you inspect and approve the job, backed by our standard 7-day warranty.

Sustainable partner commissions

We maintain sustainable operator commission tiers, ensuring drivers, dispatch couriers, and trade specialists keep the vast majority of their earnings every single week.

Frequently asked questions about pricing

Direct answers regarding fare calculations, escrow security, tolls, and payment methods.

Will my upfront ride price change if we encounter traffic on the road? +
No. Your upfront fare is locked when you confirm your ride. It will only adjust if you change your destination mid-trip, add unscheduled stops, or if vehicle waiting exceeds the initial complimentary window.
How are estate gate passes and highway tolls billed? +
Highway tolls and estate access fees are billed at actual cost as recorded by receipt. RESS never adds service charges or commissions to municipal tolls or private estate entry fees.
How does the trade service escrow protection work? +
When you accept an artisan quote in RESS Services, your payment is held securely in escrow. The artisan cannot access these funds until you tap Confirm Completion in your app. If work is unsatisfactory, our support team dispatches an independent supervisor within 24 hours to resolve the dispute or issue a refund.
What payment methods are supported in the app? +
RESS supports all major Nigerian debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Verve) processed through regulated payment gateways, direct bank transfer, USSD codes, and verified cash payments.
What is the cancellation policy? +
You can cancel any ride, food order, or artisan request within 3 minutes of booking at zero charge. If a driver or artisan has already traveled substantially toward your pickup location after the 3-minute grace window, a modest cancellation fee applies to compensate their fuel.
Why does RESS cap surge pricing during heavy rain? +
Foreign platforms often allow automated algorithms to surge fares by 300% to 400% during downpours. RESS enforces a strict surge ceiling so that stranded commuters can still afford safe transport home, while applying a reasonable incentive bonus to drivers operating in wet conditions.
