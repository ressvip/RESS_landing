How fares are calculated

Your fare reflects the distance of your trip, expected travel time, your selected ride type, and current road conditions. You'll always see the estimate before you confirm.

Dynamic demand pricing

When many people need rides at the same time, fares may rise temporarily to bring more drivers onto the road. You'll be notified if demand pricing is active.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will the price change after I book?

Your upfront fare is confirmed before you book. It only changes if you drastically change your destination, add additional stops, or if the trip takes significantly longer than expected due to unforeseen circumstances.

How do promo codes work?

If you have a promo code, enter it in the 'Promotions' section of your app profile before requesting a ride. The discount will be automatically applied to your upfront fare estimate.

Can I pay with cash?

Yes, depending on your region, you can select 'Cash' as your payment method before booking. However, we highly recommend linking a card for a seamless, contactless experience.
