Frequently asked support questions

Quick answers to common questions regarding lost property, fare reviews, artisan escrow, and corporate accounts.

How do I retrieve an item left behind in a RESS vehicle? +
Open your RESS app, navigate to Menu > Activity, select the trip in question, and tap Contact Driver. For privacy and security, the driver receives an encrypted proxy call. If more than 2 hours have passed or you cannot reach the driver, submit a ticket above with your Trip ID and our lost property unit will track the vehicle and coordinate recovery.
How long does a fare review or refund take to process? +
Our dispute desk audits route telemetry, traffic wait times, and toll receipts within 2 hours. Once an adjustment is approved, funds are credited back to your original payment method or RESS wallet balance immediately.
How does the artisan service escrow protect me if work is incomplete? +
Payments made for RESS Services remain locked in verified escrow and are never released to the technician until you test the work and tap Confirm Completion. If an artisan leaves work unfinished or disputes arise, funds remain secured while an independent RESS field supervisor evaluates the project within 24 hours.
How do businesses set up centralized monthly billing? +
Select Corporate Accounts in the form above or email info@ressvip.com. A corporate representative will configure your dashboard with consolidated monthly invoicing, departmental spend limits, and CAC-compliant electronic tax statements for corporate tax audits.
