Questions? We have answers.

What is RESS? +
RESS is an all-in-one app that brings together ride ordering, food delivery, security assistance and vehicle services, with safety at the centre of everything.
How do I sign up? +
Download the RESS app from the App Store or Google Play and create your account in the app.
Is RESS safe? +
RESS uses verification and background checks, real-time ride sharing, emergency contact alerts, safe-zone alerts and encrypted data.
Can I order food and a ride from the same app? +
Yes. RESS Ride and RESS Eat live in the same app, along with RESS Security and RESS Services.
How do I become a driver? +
Download the app and follow the driver registration steps inside it.
