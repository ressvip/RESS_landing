What you need to drive with RESS

Joining RESS is simple. Download the driver app, submit your documents for swift verification, and start taking ride requests across your city.

  • Valid driver's license: Issued by the Federal Road Safety Corps (FRSC).
  • Identity verification: Valid NIN or international passport.
  • Vehicle documents: Valid vehicle license, roadworthiness certificate, and insurance.
  • Vehicle standards: Four-door sedan, hatchback, or SUV with fully functioning air conditioning.
  • Smartphone: An active device running iOS 14 or Android 8.0 and above.

Frequently asked driver questions

Clear answers regarding payments, vehicle qualification, and platform rules.

How and when do I receive trip earnings? +
Trip earnings are credited directly to your in-app driver wallet upon ride completion. You can request instant payouts to your verified Nigerian commercial bank account at any time.
Do I face penalties if I decline a ride request? +
No. You have complete freedom to choose which trips to accept. Declining or skipping requests does not carry account suspension penalties or hidden deductions.
What vehicle requirements must my car meet? +
Vehicles must be four-door sedans, hatchbacks, or SUVs in clean condition with fully functional air conditioning, valid insurance, and roadworthiness certification.
How does safety and emergency support work during a trip? +
The RESS Driver app includes a dedicated in-app SOS emergency button. Pressing it immediately shares your live coordinates with our 24/7 emergency dispatch and alerts rapid response security units in your area.
Can I register as a fleet owner with multiple vehicles? +
Yes. Fleet owners can register multiple vehicles and assign authorized drivers through the RESS fleet portal. Contact our fleet team through our contact page to set up a commercial partner account.
