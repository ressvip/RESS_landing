Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about booking, scheduling, and traveling with RESS in Nigeria.

How do I schedule a ride in advance? +
Open the RESS app, select your destination, tap the calendar icon next to the vehicle options, and choose your desired pickup date and time up to 30 days ahead. You will receive driver confirmation details before your scheduled departure.
Can I book an airport pickup for someone else? +
Yes. When entering the pickup location, select the option to ride for a guest, enter their contact details, and their driver will call them directly upon arrival at the airport terminal. You can still track the journey live from your own app.
How do you verify drivers on RESS? +
Every driver completes identity verification using their National Identification Number (NIN), a valid driver's license check, and criminal background vetting. Vehicles must also pass physical inspection for roadworthiness before going online.
What payment options are available? +
You can pay with any valid Nigerian debit card, in-app wallet, or instant bank transfer. All fares are calculated upfront before you order, and digital receipts are generated automatically for your records.
How do I set up a corporate business account? +
Visit our business page or contact our corporate team. You can register your company, invite staff members, establish departmental budget caps, and receive consolidated monthly invoicing without manual employee expense forms.
