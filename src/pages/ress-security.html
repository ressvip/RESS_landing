Frequently asked questions

Clear details regarding panic button activation, patrol response, emergency contacts, and false alarms.

How does the volume button panic trigger function when my phone is locked? +
When enabled in app settings, RESS Security registers background accessibility permissions that detect continuous pressure on the physical volume button for 3 seconds. The signal transmits immediately without unlocking your device or illuminating the screen.
What happens if I trigger a panic alert by mistake? +
On-screen activations feature a 5-second grace window with an audible vibration. If triggered from hardware buttons, the control room calls your device immediately. You can cancel the response using your private 4-digit security PIN.
Who are the responders dispatched during an emergency? +
RESS Security partners exclusively with licensed, accredited private security patrol companies and coordinates directly with state emergency services. Responders are trained, uniformed, and equipped to manage crisis situations.
Do my emergency contacts need to have the RESS app installed? +
No. Emergency contacts receive an encrypted SMS containing a web-based tracking link that opens in any browser on any smartphone or computer, showing your real-time location and response status.
Can businesses and estate associations sign up for group coverage? +
Yes. RESS Security offers corporate and residential estate packages including dedicated patrol vehicles, static guard management, and centralized incident logs.
