Origin and Mission

Why we started RESS

Every thriving city requires infrastructure where personal safety, fair pricing, and professional accountability are built into the foundation, not treated as luxury add-ons.

When a commuter heads home across Lagos or Abuja after dark, or a family requests an emergency repair technician, an algorithm overseas cannot protect them. Only real verification, physical armed response, and local accountability can. That is why we built RESS.

The principles that guide every decision we make

We measure our success not just by the trips we complete, but by the trust we earn in the communities we serve.

Safety as the standard

Safety is not an extra charge or an afterthought. Every driver and artisan completes national identity checks and trade assessments before accepting a single job.

Fair and transparent pricing

We reject unfair surge gouging during rainfall or late hours. Pricing is transparent upfront, and funds are held in escrow until customers confirm satisfactory completion.

Empowering local partners

Drivers, food vendors, and artisans are independent business owners. We maintain sustainable, low commission structures and weekly bank payouts so our partners thrive.

Grounded in local reality

We build for the realities of Nigerian streets, estate protocols, and connectivity conditions, backed by 24/7 dedicated support teams stationed right here in Nigeria.
