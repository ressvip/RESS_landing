Before You Ride

Verified drivers and thorough background checks

Every driver on the RESS network undergoes rigorous multi-tier vetting before accepting a single passenger. We verify government-issued NIN database records, validate official driver's licenses, mandate physical roadworthiness vehicle inspections, and cross-reference background checks.

Before your car arrives, your app displays your driver's verified photograph, full legal name, community star rating, and exact vehicle make, model, and license plate number.

Frequently asked questions about safety

Details on driver vetting, panic alerts, emergency contacts, and in-trip protection protocols.

What should I do if my driver does not match the app profile? +
Do not board the vehicle. Verify the license plate, driver photo, and vehicle model before entering. If anything does not match, cancel the trip immediately in the app, select Vehicle or Driver Did Not Match, and our safety operations desk will investigate.
How does the volume button panic trigger function when my phone is locked? +
When enabled in app settings, RESS Security registers background accessibility permissions that detect continuous pressure on the physical volume button for 3 seconds. The signal transmits immediately without unlocking your device or illuminating the screen.
Do my emergency contacts need to have the RESS app installed? +
No. Emergency contacts receive an encrypted SMS containing a web-based tracking link that opens in any browser on any smartphone or computer, showing your real-time location and response status.
How are trade technicians and artisans vetted before entering private residences? +
Every artisan on RESS Services submits verified NIN records, proof of residential address, trade certification or apprenticeship credentials, and passes a mandatory physical interview. Payments remain safely locked in escrow until you approve completed work.
