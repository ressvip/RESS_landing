Frequently asked questions

Clear information on artisan screening, pricing, material purchases, and warranties.

How does RESS screen artisans before they accept jobs? +
Every artisan completes a mandatory identity verification process using their National Identity Number (NIN), provides proof of address, and undergoes practical trade skill assessments with trade guilds before their account is activated.
How are labor costs and replacement parts billed? +
Diagnostic visit fees are displayed upfront in the app. If replacement hardware or spare parts are required, the specialist submits photo receipts through the app for your digital approval before purchasing.
What if the repair or installation is unsatisfactory? +
Payment is held securely in escrow and only released after you test and confirm the repair. If an issue recurs within seven days, we dispatch the technician back to fix the problem under our 7-day workmanship guarantee.
Can I schedule a service for a future date and time? +
Yes. You can request immediate dispatch for urgent emergencies like burst pipes or power outages, or schedule an appointment for any convenient date and time up to two weeks in advance.
How can qualified artisans and contractors register with RESS? +
Trade professionals can sign up through our business portal by submitting government identification, trade credentials, and reference contacts. Onboarding is free with zero monthly subscription fees.
